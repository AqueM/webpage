## Tombs Treasures

WIP

A "tomb treasure" is something that I'd like to be buried with if my grave was to be found in the future by archeologists. Items that are beautiful and mesmerizing and potentially survive centuries buried underground. Works of art that I've fallen in love with, and would love to possess for no othe rpurpose than to appreciate them existing. 

I stumble upon them mostly through tumblr posts these days, but I try to archive them here in case tumblr finally explodes.
