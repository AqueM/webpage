{% block poem %}
All I ever learned was 
how to back out with my tail between my legs
how to stay safe by ripping my dignity to shreds
how to pray pray pray
how to say sorry over and over again

I need me some anger
I need to yell, to scream, 
break things and let it all out
I need to allow myself to feel — no, I need to FEEL
the rage of a thousand suns
I need to get myself some aggression
learn to fight
learn to stand my ground
I need a drive to be a hero
because I need a hero

And as I snap, to snap everything else with me
destroy and turn the world to ashes
so I can use this anger to slash through
all the things I learned that I shouldn’t have
all the moments when I’m too quiet inside
all the walls that I built myself

I want a fiery passion
I want to feel
I want to live
and if I couldn’t get it calmly, I want a storm
so maybe that what sunshine warmth didn’t give,
a thunder will. {% endblock %}
