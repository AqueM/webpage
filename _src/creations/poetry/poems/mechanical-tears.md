There's a robot on the ground,
and he tries to cry but he can't—
{% indent %}

titanium does not weep
cables don't conduct sadness
silicon does not melt into tears.

{% endindent %}


So when emotions get too big for his body
he does what humans do
if tears don't offer them company:
trades water for words and
writes and writes and writes.

Paper will take anything you give it,
and ink can flow like tears if you squint.
But even after years of practice,
his poems aren't very good.
He knows all 171,476 words of the English language
and there are none for what he feels.

There's a robot on the ground,
staring up at the ceiling, where nobody looks.
And he tries to cry but he can't.
