linkName: “There’s not going to be any war” 
date: 2017-02-07
lang: EN

The trembling quakes through
my soul with the rage
of a thousand suns
bright, but not the nice kind;
blinding, burning

My fragile mind shakes and shatters
a million terrified pieces
behind the impassible wall of apathy

I do not fall. I rise.
Phoenixes are of their own ashes.


They will not take me alive
nor without my human rights.
even if all I can do
is write poems
about how easily people forget
about how history repeats itself
about being like my grandmother on her dying bed
— speaking in foreign language
of violence, soldiers and fear
about the fact that
This Is Already War.
