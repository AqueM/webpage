Dear brain,
I’ve had enough. 
I’m not taking the best care of myself, 
but I’ve seen people do worse, and live with it.
I’m not eating pure organic vegan diet,
I’m not excercising daily,
I’m not meditating every evening.
But neither is half the population, 
and I don’t see them having problems
to get out of bed after a good night’s sleep
to make dinner for themselves,
to watch a cartoon without their mind turning off
like a cellphone lying about still having 30% battery
and suddenly deciding to die in the middle of a call. 
I’ve had enough. I’m done. We’re done. 
I know I can. 
I’ve been through worse things
every fullmoon every month. 
I’ve stopped believing in what you say,
and starting today
I’m going to drag myself out of bed
because the calories are there, 
you just somehow refuse to follow orders.
I’ve stopped believing your false reports.
There’s a war to be won, soldier, 
a world to conquer, mate,
and damsels to save, my page. 
We ain’t staying home.

Dear shadow,
We survived. 
But tell me what,
because you stole the memories,
and I just have a vague idea of battles long gone. 
Tell me what,
so I can learn from history, 
and prepare for what life will throw at me.
Tell me what,
so I can weep,
and forget.
Let us finally sit down and relax. 
We’re not dying today.
Nor tomorrow or the day after.
We’re going to get old and crusty and
— if you just let me —
happy.
Stop resisting like a caged animal,
like cornered prey. I’m no longer five.
I grew up and can fend for myself,
and I promise, I can defend you too.
Let us finally sit down and relax,
tell me where it hurts,
I know first aid,
and if you need a better medic, I’ll get some,
just show me where it hurts,
so I can heal us. 

There’s a war to be won,
and I’ve waged it against myself
because you two keep the real enemy hidden,
if there even is one.
So dear shadow, dear brain:
Fuck you. Work with me. {% endblock %}
