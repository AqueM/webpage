{% block poem %}hold it together just
a little more
hold it together, with glue if you must
don’t complain that you’re sore

if you bend, you break
you know that well enough
if you don’t ignore the ache
they’ll see through your bluff


the heart that hurts is not a lethal wound
the thoughts that itch need to be pruned


hold it together, with strings if so be it
though you fall apart bit by bit
hold it together, alone, on your own
otherwise, you’re dragging everybody down
{% endblock %}
