{% block poem %}
People think that we, those with absent faces,
are silent.
But I talk to people a lot
Just they can’t don’t hear (could if we tried, I think)

Like that lady at the station —
complimented her beautiful
{% indent %}patterned

{% indent %}autumn

{% indent %}violet

{% indent %}floral{% endindent %}{% endindent %}{% endindent %}{% endindent %}

coat;
but she never heard,
maybe she’d have smiled.
Like that man who always takes the same train as me
in the morning —
{% indent %}

asked him if he likes comic books,
he always has that Superman shirt{% endindent %}

{% indent %}

what music he listens to,
with those big headphones;{% endindent %}

but he never answers,
maybe we’d have became commuting buddies.
Like that girl in the evening train, stuffed in a corner seat
with big glasses and a messy braid —
I was her mentor over the comic she was drawing

{% indent %}

'the nose a little too long, maybe
            you’d want a softer pencil to
            bring the darks out’;{% endindent %}

I think she knew — saw how I watched

{% indent %} 

her hand
her strokes{% endindent %}

pretending I was looking out the window
maybe I even smiled a bit, who knows;
I think she answered me
{% indent %}

with a frightened stare, but{% endindent %}

we got no conclusions out of this

{% indent %}

anxious conversation.{% endindent %}

Like the Professor from my university
one who taught me modern philosophy and
{% indent %}

middle-eastern affirmations and
eloquent humour that I never use{% endindent %}

one I meet every-so-often — he must live around—
‘How are you, Professor’
'Do you want me to help with those bags, Professor’
'I read a book on psychology, I think you’d like it, Professor’
'That post on your social feed was great, Professor’;
And at this point I’m not sure
how to ask, between the

{% indent %}

casual conversation and the noisy silence{% endindent %}

'Professor, do you even remember me?’

So I do, I do indeed talk a lot
but only the stuffed animals

{% indent %}

and
the trees in the park
and
the train seat{% endindent %}
hear.
