Nobody thought twice about it
even me.
Just lonely, just solitary, just nerd.
Nobody thought to stop for a moment
and wonder if they’re right about it
even me.
It’s a blessing, for a time,
before I start to wonder
Nobody thought to call me broken
even me.


For years
I thought that it was all but
a game,
a clever marketing ruse, a world-wide meme,
a distraction to the difficulties of world
and learned to treat it as such,
laugh at the horny jokes, admire sexy curves
and everybody thought that this means
I feel these, I feel it, I am it
even me.


But then doubts start
and as I grow up, and down and to the sides
and in all other directions, including inside
Somebody thought about it
and it is me.
Is this how others feel? Is this lonely, nerdy?
Or is it broken? Weird?
Unnatural?


For years
I’m torn between
not thinking twice about it
and thinking ten thousand times.
Nobody has an answer
even me.
