What is justice if not a coward’s excuse
blindfold for repayment long overdue?
What is justice but vengeance that befits you
a sword dulled with grandiloquent values?

Hope is not a damsel in distress,
an ephemeral thing made of whispers
{% indent %} and spiderwebs.{% endindent %}

She has blood on her knuckles, grit in her teeth
and a sword she’ll never sheathe.

I will not idly stand by
while our grains wither and our people die.
I will not apologise for swallowing the sun
instead of watching the world burn.

What is a monster but a child wronged
bitter seeds blooming into sour crops?
What is a monster if not a man that was broken
angry thorns in paw pads still heartachingly soft?

Even the sun eater deserves a second chance
if you have loved him but once.
