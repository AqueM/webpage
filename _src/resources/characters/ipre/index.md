<abbr title="Interplanar Partnership for Research and Exploration">IPRE</abbr> is a casual organization of scholars and academics. The members do not hail from one school or discipline, but rather cover a variety of fields, and are simply loosely united by their desire for more cooperation between the intellectuals of the Planes.

The organization doesn't have any official headquarters or point of contact, there's no statute or an official procedure to join except "befriend one of the members and start calling yourself one of them". As such, it is more of a clique than an institution, and some rumors say it was established purely for the group to sound "more official and prestigious", and have an easier time getting funding for their projects.
