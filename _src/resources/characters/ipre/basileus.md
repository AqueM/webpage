An elderly high elf wizard that lives a day's travel west of [Zelbross](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Zelbross){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"} in central Swords Coast with his apprentice, Mol Deihram. He is a biologist, which is evident by his abode — a tower shaped from a giant tree. His main income source is being an on-call wizard for the local population, earning him respect and renown, even if he fixes most problems by turning the issue into a herd of rabbits with his Wand of True Polymorph. 

Personally, he's very annoying to deal with, as he is rather arrogant, self-obsessed and has an inflated sense of his ego. If one manages to look past that, though, and assuming his attention can be forced away from his academic studies, he's a generally well-intentioned person.  If it doesn’t inconvenience him too much, he will try to help others, but he will also loudly complain of their limited minds and their inability to comprehend concepts as simple as poly-planal transweave theory. He also tends to approach everything from the perspective of someone with centuries of experience and doesn't seem to grasp that some people have much shorter lifespans than elves. That particular thing is especially distressing for Mol Deihram, who is a human but has been under his tutelage for two decades now — a time far too long for his liking, a fact that Basileus seems to willfully ignore.
