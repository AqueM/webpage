Doctor Landox is a human that comes from Tashluta, where the Temple of the All-Seeing Orb inspired him to get interested in stars. He is more of a scientist than a diviner, though, and ended up studying the celestial movements not to decipher their prophetic meaning, but to discover the laws governing them. For that reason he moved to northern Sword Coast, hoping to join the Academy of Stargazers, only to be rejected from the all-female school. 

He eventually settled down at the [College of the Herald](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/College_of_the_Herald){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"}, where he learned of and became heavily influenced by the work of [Elmandar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Elmandar){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"}, an astronomer from Bral, a city in the Tears of Selune. His life's mission became to create accurate star maps with which people of Faerun could navigate the seas just like spelljammers navigated the sky. Chasing that goal, he investigated a recent abnormality in star behavior by traveling to the site of a meteor crater in the far North, which is the last time anyone has ever heard of him.

According to his coworkers at the College of the Herald, he is a surprisingly sober-minded person for someone who grew up among diviners. He's known to be a bit scatter-brained, though, and often employed the help of his familiar, **Mayonnaise**, to keep track of dates and events for him.
