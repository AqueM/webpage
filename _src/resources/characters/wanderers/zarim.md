Zarim was a relatively normal orc from [Kingdom of Many-Arrows](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Many-Arrows){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"}, shunned by his parents and peers for his tendency to appreciate the beauty of the world — sunsets, flowers, art etc. When he was 10, an errant knight with a Ruby Rose symbol on his chest "won" him by defeating Zarim's father in a duel. But when the boy arrived at the nearby camp of Sunnite missionaries as ordered, expecting a life of servitude, it turned out nobody there knew anything of the knight. 

Seeing this as a sign from the goddess herself, Zarim joined the cult and returned with them to [Waterdeep](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Waterdeep){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"}, where he settled and eventually became a paladin of [Sune](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sune){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"} and a member of the [Order of the Ruby Rose](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sisters_and_Brothers_of_the_Ruby_Rose){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"} himself. He does not know who the knight that freed him was — a Sunnite looking to do good and not wanting any attention? An emmissary of the goddess herself? (Or perhaps even himself from another time and place?) — but he is thankful to them all the same and wants be that kind of person to someone else. For this reason, he helps anyone and everyone, in any way he can. Whenever it is talking to them, volunteering his time, or his able body in whatever way is needed. 

It is through the waterdhavian temple of Sune that he met and befriended [Irisse Vanima](/resources/characters/ipre/irisse), who helped him get accustomed to the big city and human culture.

He is a gentle and kind person who will always stand to protect the weak and helpless. He tries to solve problems without violence, but understands that sometimes it is unavoidable — and that as an orc, he is uniquely suited to employ overwhelming physical power to end conflicts swiftly.
