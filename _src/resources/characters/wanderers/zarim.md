{% heading "h2", "History" %}

Zarim was a relatively normal son of Ohrug "Rib Bruiser" and Avez the Proud, orcs of the [Kingdom of Many-Arrows](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Many-Arrows){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"}. Despite being just as good as brothers with a weapon, he was shunned by his parents and peers for his tendency to appreciate the beauty of the world — sunsets, flowers, art etc. 

His family often visited the neighbouring human settlements of Luruar to trade, which Zarim used as an excuse to spend as much time away from his abusive parents. That's how he encountered a small group of [Sunnites](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sune){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"} in [Silverymoon](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Silverymoon){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"}, with which he made quick friends. He planned to establish a trading 'outpost' in a city for his tribe once he comes of age at 12, the only way he saw to distance himself from his family without having to fight his father for dominance. 

Yet when he was 10, while his oldest brother was tormenting him, a fully-armored knight with a Ruby Rose symbol on his chest intervened, announcing that Zarim did nothing to deserve this treatment. Zarim's father attacked the knight to assert his right to do whatever he pleases with his children, but promptly lost. To everyone's surprise, when the knight wanted to kill his opponent, Zarim begged for his father's life; despite the years of abuse, this was still his family. Moved by this, the knight agreed to spare Ohrug. Instead they demanded that Zarim would be granted to them as a reward for the duel they won. Ohrug had no other choice but to agree, and the stranger told Zarim to gather his things and meet him at the nearby camp of Sunnite missionaries.

When Zarim finished saying goodbyes to his family and arrived at the camp, expecting a life of servitude, it turned out the knight was not there and nobody knew of such a person. Seeing his newfound freedom as a sign from the goddess herself, Zarim joined the cult and returned with them to [Waterdeep](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Waterdeep){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"}, where he settled and eventually became a paladin of Sune and a member of [Order of the Ruby Rose](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Sisters_and_Brothers_of_the_Ruby_Rose){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"} himself. He does not know who the knight that freed him was — a Sunnite looking to do good and not wanting any attention? An emmissary of the goddess herself? (Or perhaps even himself from another time and place?) — but he is thankful to them all the same and wants be that kind of person to someone else. For this reason, he helps anyone and everyone, in any way he can. Whenever it is talking to them, volunteering his time, or his able body in whatever way is needed. 

It is through the waterdhavian temple of Sune that he met and befriended [Irisse Vanima](/resources/characters/ipre/irisse), who helped him get accustomed to the big city and human culture.

{% heading "h2", "Description" %}

Zarim is a tall, muscular orc that is surprisingly gentle and constantly smiling. He always wears a tabard with the holy symbol of Sune, and is extremely friendly. His black hair is long and messy, with copious amounts of grey streaks. He wears plenty of jewelry and decorates himself with tattoos and skin-paint often. He is a gentle and kind person who will always stand to protect the weak and helpless. He tries to solve problems without violence, but understands that sometimes it is unavoidable — and that as an orc, he is uniquely suited to employ overwhelming physical power to end conflicts swiftly.
