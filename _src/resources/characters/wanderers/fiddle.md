Rikas is a peasant that escaped servitude on[Bral](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Bral){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"} by stowing on a spelljammer and coming to [Waterdeep](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Waterdeep){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"}.  There, thanks to his skill with the fiddle, he ended up befriending students of the [College of New Olamn](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/New_Olamn){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"}. They hung out while the fledgling bards practiced, which enabled Fiddle to pick up a few skills on his own, too. Though he has never officially studied at any bard college, he calls himself a bard these days, and his passion for music and knowledge managed to get him hired at the [College of the Herald](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/College_of_the_Herald){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"} as a junior archivist. That is where he met [Sarr Landox](/resources/characters/ipre/sarr) and told him about the famed star charts of [Elmandar](https://forgottenrealms.fandom.com/wiki/Elmandar){target=_blank}{rel="external nofollow"}, setting off Sarr's fascination.

Fiddle is a rather laid-back person, phlegmatic even, and most would consider him a bit bland or even boring. That changes completely when he's performing with his instrument — it is almost as if the devils take him over, and imbue him with impressive skill and charisma.
